Local MD and PIMD Viewer

This page runs entirely from local files. Upload either a multi-frame XYZ trajectory for standard MD, or a standardized PIMD H5 file with bead-resolved coordinates and system potential energy, then inspect the motion in 3D without loading a PKL dataset.
